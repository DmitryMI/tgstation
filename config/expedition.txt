# Expedition module configuration

## Whether Expedition vessel content and Expedition ghost roles are available.
EXPEDITION_ENABLED 1

## Expedition vessel shuttle ignition and call time, in deciseconds.
EXPEDITION_IGNITION_TIME 10
EXPEDITION_CALL_TIME 10

## Expedition navigation destination designation time, in deciseconds.
EXPEDITION_DESIGNATE_TIME 5

## Display configured z-level names in Expedition navigation instead of sector numbers.
EXPEDITION_USE_Z_LEVEL_REAL_NAMES 0

## Give all non-Commander Expedition crew equal access to the vessel outside its cockpit.
EXPEDITION_BAYS_SHARED_ACCESS 0

## Give all Expedition crew access to the cockpit and communications console.
EXPEDITION_COCKPIT_ALL_ACCESS 0

## Maximum number of ruin objectives included in an Expedition mission report.
EXPEDITION_MAX_OBJECTIVES 5
